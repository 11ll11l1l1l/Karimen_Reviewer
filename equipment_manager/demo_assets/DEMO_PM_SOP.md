# DEMO SOP — Preventive Maintenance

Demo/test document only. Do not use as a production procedure.

1. Place equipment in the controlled maintenance state.
2. Verify LOTO/safety requirements and required parts.
3. Inspect chamber, vacuum seals, utilities and interlocks.
4. Record measurements in the PM execution workspace.
5. Escalate any out-of-specification result through Incident / RCA.
6. Complete qualification and return-to-service workflow when required.
